# First-challenge boss movesets after ability preparation

The eight naturally changed sets from the ability-preparation patch now have explicit, approved move overrides. Only these eight Pokémon in the first-challenge teams change. Trainer species, levels, team sizes, all other party members and rematch overrides remain unchanged.

| Trainer | Pokémon | Level | Approved moves |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Erika | Weepinbell | 32 | Sleep Powder / Sludge / Razor Leaf / Mega Drain |
| Sabrina | Drowzee | 39 | Psybeam / Mud Wave / Hypnosis / Reflect |
| Blaine | Magmar | 40 | Will-O-Wisp / Fire Punch / Mega Punch / Fire Blast |
| Lorelei | Cloyster | 53 | Ice Beam / Surf / Spike Cannon / Shell Smash |
| Bruno | Machamp | 58 | Bulk Up / Triple Kick / Pierce / Rock Slide |
| Bruno | Poliwrath | 57 | Bulk Up / Drain Punch / Thunderpunch / Hydro Pump |
| Bruno | Pinsir | 56 | X-Scissor / Lunge / Submission / Rock Tomb |
| Agatha | Marowak | 55 | Wailing Cry / Bonemerang / Will-O-Wisp / Curse |

## Intended roles

- Weepinbell gains draining recovery instead of redundant Poisonpowder; Sludge retains a chance to poison, and Razor Leaf supplies direct pressure.
- Drowzee uses both of its types, sleep and physical protection. It retains its unevolved stats to balance the disruptive kit.
- Magmar combines reliable Fire Punch, stronger Fire Blast, Fighting-type Mega Punch and burn support.
- Cloyster restores Water coverage. Ice Beam is its reliable Ice attack; Shell Smash boosts Attack and Speed for its physical Rock-type Spike Cannon while reducing Defense.
- Machamp gains a multi-hit attack, setup and Flying coverage. Pierce remains its defense-bypassing option. This prepares its moveset for future ability work without adding an ability here.
- Poliwrath has a physical Fighting attack again: Bulk Up boosts Drain Punch and Defense, while the drain supplies recovery. Thunderpunch and Hydro Pump remain special attacks and receive no damage boost from Bulk Up.
- Pinsir uses priority Attack reduction through Lunge, Speed reduction through Rock Tomb, high-crit Bug damage through X-Scissor and strong Fighting coverage through Submission. It has a different role from Machamp's setup kit.
- Marowak restores Ghost STAB through Wailing Cry, which also lowers Special. Burn weakens physical opponents; Curse adds residual pressure and Bonemerang covers Normal Pokémon immune to Curse. It has no healing move in this set.

All selected moves are legal under the species' current level-up and TM/HM lists at the listed battle levels. Each set is pinned explicitly rather than depending on the latest four naturally learned moves. Engine validation loads every trainer team, verifies these exact sets and checks their PP on send-out in both Red and Blue.

Species learnsets and the complete [movepool document](ALL-POKEMON-MOVEPOOLS.md) are unchanged by this boss-only patch. Existing saves remain compatible; these sets take effect when the corresponding first-challenge battle is generated.
