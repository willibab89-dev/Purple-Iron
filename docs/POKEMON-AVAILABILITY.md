# Pokémon availability — current game

[CSV spreadsheet](POKEMON-AVAILABILITY.csv) · [Offline searchable HTML](POKEMON-AVAILABILITY.html) (download and open in a browser).

Current implemented game, including the repaired Mew diploma gate. The route encounter redesign is implemented. All 183 species are listed. The revised Gym/Elite Four teams and post-Hall-of-Fame Gym/League rematches are included; Erika’s unique Tangela and Venusaur appear in her postgame rematch. Rates assume no Repel or shiny-attraction override and are conditional on a generated encounter/successful bite, not per step/cast; custom replacement pools are included in normal grass/surf/Super Rod rates. All fishing requires facing valid water from land; fishing while surfing is blocked. Old/Good Rod generic entries apply in fishable water; Good Rod’s two normal species are ordinarily 50/50 before applicable custom replacement pools. Post-Champion Safari expansion has a 26/256 activation roll before custom pools; its species-selection loop is not a uniform per-species distribution, so exact individual expansion rates are not claimed. It retains the underlying encounter level. Safari expansion contains original species only. Its lists share a final terminator: Center can draw all 146 nonlegendary originals; East draws middle evolutions plus the following groups; North draws nonevolving plus final-stage species; West draws final-stage species. Safari base-table rates shown are pre-Champion; expansion changes them after the Champion. Special postgame Ditto fishing levels above 100 are shown as implemented. Mew requires the diploma for 150 original non-Mew species. Before the diploma, the same level-70 slot gives Ditto; these two conditional entries are alternatives, not simultaneous odds. NPC trades include only actively referenced scripts; the unused Butterfree→Beedrill record is not counted. Gifts and fossil choices may be mutually exclusive before later alternatives. “Before Champion” describes a species individually, not completion of every choice simultaneously. Fixed encounters listed here are capturable; the Pokémon Tower ghost Marowak is excluded. Route rates include the shared ordinary-rare and unique-species replacement pools.

173 obtainable, 2 postgame / gated, 8 trainer-only.

## Availability gaps to review

175 species have a player-acquisition route in each version. Eight variants are trainer-only: unique Blastoise, Koga’s Grimer, Erika’s Ivysaur, Giovanni’s Nidoking, Brock’s Onix, Surge’s Raichu, Erika’s Tangela and Erika’s Venusaur. The latter two appear in Erika’s post-Hall-of-Fame rematch at levels 62 and 70; they are no longer unassigned. All eight lack postgame Safari/evolution access. Mewtwo requires postgame Cerulean Cave access; Mew additionally requires the original-150 diploma.
