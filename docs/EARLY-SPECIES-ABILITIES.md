# Abilities: starters and early Bug lines

Implemented for 100 species, with five equally likely slots per species. The saved slot stays the same on evolution; the ability comes from the evolved species' corresponding slot. Existing saves and the fixed party/box record sizes remain compatible. Unique variants do not inherit these pools automatically.

## Effects

Krabby and Kingler effects: Crushing Claws gives physical attacks a 20% chance to lower the target’s Defense. Shell Armor prevents critical hits. Brute Force boosts physical moves with base power at most 60 by 30%. Water Pressure boosts Water damage by 2% per remaining PP, up to 50%. Hyper Cutter prevents Attack drops and boosts critical-hit damage by 25%.

Drowzee and Hypno effects: Nightmare damages a sleeping opponent for 1/16 maximum HP at round end while the holder is active. Sandman gives successful Ground attacks a 20% chance to put the target to sleep. Deep Sleep extends newly inflicted sleep by one turn, including at the maximum sleep counter. Dreamcatcher heals 1/8 maximum HP after newly putting an opponent to sleep. Mental Shield reduces incoming special damage by 20%.

Onix effects: Crushing Weight boosts Rock damage by 20% when the user’s Defense is higher than the target’s. Colossal Size multiplies maximum HP by 1.25 and Speed by 0.75, including stored stats and capsule changes. Onix’s Sturdy survives a lethal hit from full HP once per battle; Geodude’s existing Sturdy remains unchanged. Earthshaker gives successful Ground attacks a 20% chance to lower the target’s Speed. Earth Eater absorbs a successful Ground attack and heals 1/4 maximum HP, subject to the usual healing cap.

Gastly-family effects: Levitate blocks Ground moves. Gas Body gives incoming physical attacks a 30% chance to lower the attacker’s Accuracy. Prankster gives status moves +1 priority. Nocturnal boosts damage by 20% against sleeping opponents. Mind Games raises Special one stage after newly confusing the opponent, once per battle. Cursed Touch gives incoming physical attacks a 20% chance to curse the attacker. Cursed Body gives damaging hits a 30% chance to disable the attack used for 1–8 turns; copied attacks absent from the attacker’s moveset cannot be disabled.

Shellder and Cloyster effects: Shell Armor prevents critical hits. Skill Link makes multi-hit moves hit their maximum number of times and guarantees Triple Kick’s follow-up hits after the first connects. Spiked Shell damages physical attackers for 1/16 of their maximum HP per hit. Deepsea Pearl reduces special damage by 20%. Liquidate changes damaging Normal moves to Water and boosts their damage by 20%, including moves called through Metronome or Mirror Move.

Grimer and Muk effects: Contamination gives successful Ground attacks a 30% chance to poison. Toxic Waste heals 1/16 maximum HP at round end if either living Pokémon is poisoned; Gloom’s version continues to check its opponent only. Sticky Body gives incoming physical attacks a 20% chance to lower the attacker’s Speed one stage. Liquid Ooze damages Pokémon that drain the holder instead of healing them. Soft Body reduces incoming Normal and Fighting damage by 25%.

Seel and Dewgong effects: Blubber halves Fire damage and multiplies maximum HP by 1.2. Hydration heals 1/8 maximum HP after a Water move. Refrigerate changes damaging Normal moves to Ice and boosts their damage by 20%; this also applies to moves called by Metronome or Mirror Move. Icebreaker makes Ice attacks super effective against Water types, combining normally with a second type. Snow Veil raises Evasion one stage after an Ice move, once per battle.

Doduo and Dodrio effects: Twin Beaks doubles move secondary-effect chances. Triple Threat makes variable multi-hit moves hit at least three times and guarantees Triple Kick’s follow-up hits after the first connects; moves limited to two hits stay at two. Stampede boosts Normal damage by 20% when faster than the target. Quick Feet raises Speed one stage upon a major status condition. Relentless raises damage by 10% per previous consecutive successful damaging attack, up to 30%, and resets on a miss, status move or switching out. Headstrong prevents confusion and reduces Psychic damage by 25%.

Farfetch’d effects: Underdog boosts damage by 20% against a Pokémon with a higher total of the five base stats. Unyielding prevents opponents from lowering Attack or Defense. Tasty Leek heals 1/10 maximum HP per critical hit. Precision gives moves with 95 base accuracy 100 base accuracy and boosts their damage by 20%. Razor Edge adds 15 percentage points to the critical-hit chance of Cut, Slash, X-Scissor, Razor Leaf and Razor Wind.

Magnemite-family effects: Levitate grants Ground immunity. Battery boosts Electric damage by 20% while the move has more than half its maximum PP before use, including PP Ups. Overload raises Special once per battle after receiving a special attack. Chain Reaction makes the next Electric attack deal 20% more damage after using an Electric attack; it stays armed through other moves and resets on switching. Magnetic Field prevents the opposing Pokémon from raising Speed or Evasion while its holder is active.

Slowpoke-family effects: Shell Armor prevents critical hits. Oblivious prevents confusion and flinching. Unaware ignores positive Attack and Special stages when calculating incoming move damage, while retaining negative stages and status penalties. Patient boosts move damage by 20% when the opponent has already acted during the round. Simple doubles positive and negative stat-stage changes, subject to normal caps.

Ponyta-family effects: Flame Body gives incoming physical hits a 20% chance to burn the attacker. Competitive raises Attack once per battle when an opponent raises a stat. Blazing Trail boosts Fire damage by 20% when the holder is faster. Wild Charge raises Speed after using a damaging physical move once per battle. Stamina raises Defense after each hit received, including individual hits of a multi-hit attack.

Geodude-family effects: Levitate blocks Ground moves. Immunity prevents poison and clears existing poison on entry or when gained by capsule. Sturdy leaves the holder at 1 HP when a direct hit would otherwise knock it out from full HP. Rollout boosts Ground and Rock attacks by 10% per prior consecutive successful Ground or Rock attack; a miss, different move or switch resets the streak. The counter saturates at 255 consecutive attacks. Heavyweight boosts Rock damage by 20% when the holder’s Defense exceeds the target’s Defense. Digger boosts damage by 25% against Ground types.

Tentacool-family effects: Clear Body prevents opposing stat reductions. Liquid Ooze damages the attacker by the amount a draining move would restore, including Leech Seed; Magic Guard prevents this damage. Stinging Skin gives incoming physical hits a 20% chance to poison the attacker. Jelly Body reduces physical damage by 20%. Venomous increases damage by 25% against poisoned targets.

Bellsprout-family effects: Acidic gives Poison hits a 25% chance to lower Defense. Carnivorous heals 20% of maximum HP after a direct knockout. Sticky Trap gives incoming physical hits a 20% chance to lower the attacker’s Speed. Chlorophyll increases Speed by 50% below one-third HP. Digestive Acid boosts Poison damage by 25% if the target’s Defense exceeds its Special.

Machop-family effects: No Guard makes moves used by or against the holder bypass accuracy and Fly/Dig evasion, while type and ability immunities still apply. Guts adds an Attack stage while a major status condition is present. Iron Grip boosts physical damage by 20% against higher Defense than the holder’s Attack. Four Arms makes variable-hit moves hit five times and prevents subsequent Triple Kick accuracy failures. Grappler gives physical hits a 10% chance to lower Defense. Bodybuilder raises Defense after using a Fighting move once per battle. Machamp learns Barrage at level 50; Barrage is a variable-hit Grass-type move in this game.

Abra-family effects: Run Away guarantees escape from wild battles. Mental Shield reduces special damage by 20%. Telepathy raises Evasion after using a Psychic move once per battle. Genius adds 15 percentage points of accuracy to status moves. Magic Bounce reflects opposing status moves aimed at its holder, once per move. Spoon Bender makes Psychic attacks ignore 20% of the target’s Special. Magic Guard prevents indirect damage, and Mind Reader prevents Accuracy drops.

Poliwag-family effects: Hypnotic adds 15 percentage points of accuracy to sleep-inducing moves. Slippery raises Evasion after a physical hit once per battle. Swift Swim raises Speed two stages after a Water hit. Hydration heals 1/8 maximum HP after using a Water move. Water Veil prevents burns and halves Fire damage. Wrestler gives Fighting hits a 20% chance to paralyze. Power Swim raises Attack after using a Water move once per battle. Iron Fist boosts punching-move damage by 25%.

Growlithe-family effects: Loyalty heals 20% of maximum HP after a direct knockout. Legendary boosts damage by 25% when the move’s maximum PP, including PP Ups, is at most 10. Momentum raises Speed after a recoil move once per battle. Steadfast prevents confusion and flinching. Intimidate lowers the opponent’s Speed once per holder per battle. Elegant Mane reduces damage from special attacks by 20%.

Mankey-family effects: Defiant prevents the Attack, Speed and Special penalties caused by burn, paralysis and poison; other effects of those conditions remain. Short Temper raises Attack after a miss. Scrappy allows Normal and Fighting attacks to hit Ghost types while retaining the target’s other type matchup. Unsophisticated increases damage of moves with listed 100% accuracy by 25% and lowers their accuracy by 10 percentage points. Bloodlust boosts physical damage by 25% against opponents below half HP.

Psyduck-family effects: Headache raises Special after a special hit once per battle. Psionic boosts Psychic damage by 20%. Damp grants immunity to Explosion and Selfdestruct and halves Fire damage. Confounding gives Psychic hits a 20% chance to confuse. Unstable raises a random battle stat once below half HP. Cloud Nine blocks stat drops from opponents’ moves. Swift Swim raises Speed two stages after a Water hit. Mind Reader prevents Accuracy drops. Water Veil prevents burns and halves Fire damage.

Meowth-family effects: Opportunist increases move damage by 20% if any opposing stat stage is below neutral. Greedy doubles trainer prize money if a holder participated in the battle; Pay Day money is separate. Resourceful increases the maximum PP of each move by 5, capped at the game’s 63-PP storage limit. Quick Reflexes gives the first damaging move after entry +1 priority. Technician boosts damaging moves with listed base power at most 60 by 50%; fixed-damage moves are excluded.

Diglett-family effects: Ambush increases the first damaging move after entry by 25%, including fixed damage and all hits of a multi-hit move. A miss or immunity consumes it; a charging move consumes it when the attack executes. Subterranean reduces super-effective damage by 25%. Elusive raises Evasion on entry and removes that stage after the first opposing damaging move executes, including a miss or a hit on Substitute; status moves do not consume it. Aftershock gives Ground hits a 20% chance to paralyze, subject to existing status immunity, Substitute and Shield Dust. Sand Rush raises Speed one stage after a Ground move executes, once per battle.

Venonat-family effects: Shield Dust blocks added effects of incoming attacks. Toxic Scales gives each physical hit a 30% chance to inflict ordinary poison, subject to existing status and type immunities. Tinted Lens doubles damage from resisted attacks without changing type effectiveness or immunity. Poison Absorb heals 1/8 maximum HP each turn while the opposing active Pokémon is alive and poisoned. Venomous increases move damage by 25% against poisoned targets, including fixed-damage moves.

Paras-family effects: Effect Spore gives each physical hit a 20% chance to inflict a randomly selected poison, paralysis or sleep. Leeching heals 10% of physical damage dealt per hit, rounded down with a minimum of 1; damage to Substitute is capped at its remaining HP. Parasite changes a contact attacker’s active ability to Parasite until it switches. Mycelium bypasses accuracy checks for status moves while retaining other failure and immunity rules. Regrowth heals 1/16 maximum HP each turn. When Parasect is knocked out by a direct attack, Death Spore chooses Curse or ordinary poison equally; poison follows existing type and status immunity rules.

Oddish-family effects: Perfume raises Evasion one stage on entry. Absorption adds 25% of damage dealt to healing from damaging drain moves. Pollen adds 20 accuracy points to Poisonpowder, Stun Spore, Sleep Powder and Spore. Effect Spore gives each physical hit a 20% chance to choose poison, paralysis or sleep equally; existing status immunities apply. Stench reduces damage from poisoned opponents by 25%. Toxic Waste heals 1/16 maximum HP each turn while the opposing active Pokémon is alive and poisoned. Photosynthesis heals 1/16 maximum HP each turn while above half HP.

Zubat-family effects: Echolocation adds 20 percentage points to move accuracy before the final cap. Bloodsucker adds 25% of damage dealt to healing from draining moves, rounded down. Gale Wings gives Flying moves +1 priority at full HP. Infiltrator bypasses opposing Reflect, Light Screen and Substitute while preserving them for later attacks; screen-breaking moves still remove screens. Predator boosts physical damage by 20% against slower opponents.

Jigglypuff-family effects: Inflatable raises Defense one stage after each physical hit received; hits on a Substitute do not count. Performer multiplies sound-move accuracy by 1.2. Rubber Skin grants immunity to opposing Electric moves, including Thunder Wave. Soothing Voice restores 1/8 maximum HP after a sound move executes, including when it misses; it cannot revive. Thick Fat halves damage from Fire and Ice moves. Sound moves are Growl, Roar, Sing, Supersonic, Screech, Wailing Cry, Snarl and Heal Bell.

Vulpix-family effects: Aftermath curses a living opponent whose direct attack knocks the holder out. Foxfire raises Special one stage after inflicting burn, once per battle. Nine Lives survives a lethal direct attack with 1 HP once per battle. Fox's Cunning raises Evasion one stage after using a Ghost move, once per battle. Illusion halves damage from the first damaging attack received after entry, including all hits of a multi-hit attack. Hits on a Substitute do not consume Illusion or Nine Lives.

Clefairy-family effects: Lucky Star gives damaging moves a 10% chance to deal 50% more damage. Magic Guard prevents indirect damage from poison, burn, Leech Seed, recoil, crash damage, Spiny Armor and residual Wrap, Fire Spin and Curse. Moon Child heals 1/16 maximum HP each turn, with a minimum of 1 HP and no revival. Unpredictable boosts damage from moves called by Metronome by 30%. Wonder Skin halves the accuracy of incoming status moves.

Nidoran♂-family effects: Poison Point has a 25% chance to poison physical attackers. Piercing Horn ignores 20% of Defense with physical moves. Sheer Force increases damage by 20% for moves with secondary effects and suppresses those effects. Bloodlust increases physical damage by 25% against a living target below half HP. Rampage increases physical damage by 25% and inflicts recoil equal to 10% of damage dealt per hit, rounded down with a minimum of 1; Substitute damage is capped at its remaining HP. Fixed physical damage is affected by Bloodlust and Rampage.

Nidoran♀-family effects: Poison Point has a 25% chance to poison the attacker after a physical hit, including ranged attacks; Weedle retains its previously implemented contact-only Poison Point. Measured multiplies outgoing move damage by 0.8, including fixed-damage moves and Counter, and doubles the probability of move secondary effects using the existing single RNG draw, capped at certainty. Thick Hide multiplies incoming physical move damage by 0.85, including fixed damage. Protective Instinct raises Defense one stage below half HP once per battle. Iron Will prevents critical hits, including guaranteed critical hits. Existing Substitute, type/status immunity and minimum-damage rules apply.

Sandshrew-family effects: Rolling Defense raises Defense one stage after executing a Normal move, once per battle. Spiny Armor damages a physical attacker by 1/16 of its maximum HP per direct hit, including ranged attacks and hits that KO the holder; it does not activate through Substitute. Sand Armor reduces super-effective damage by 25%. Burrower restores 1/8 maximum HP after executing a Ground move. Sharp Claws multiplies physical critical-hit chance by 1.5. Sand Veil raises Evasion one stage after executing a Ground move, once per battle. The Normal/Ground triggers apply when the move executes, including on a miss; two-turn moves trigger when their attack executes.

Pikachu-family effects: Static has an exact 20% chance to paralyze an attacker after a physical hit, including ranged physical moves, once per move; the existing Electric-type/status immunities and Substitute rules apply. Voltage increases Electric damage by 20% against paralyzed targets. Overcharge raises Special one stage after a successful critical hit, once per battle; a hit on a Substitute counts. Quick Reflexes gives the first attempted damaging move after entry +1 priority. Quick Feet raises Speed one stage when a major status is present; it can trigger again after the status is cured and a new one is inflicted, and reapplies on entry. Paralysis retains its existing Speed penalty.

Ekans-family effects: Intimidate lowers the opposing Pokémon’s Speed one stage on entry, once per holder per battle; a given target can only be affected once per battle, so the effect does not stack. Shed Skin has an exact 30% chance at turn end to cure a major status and clear Toxic counters, recalculating stats and synchronizing the party. Venomous increases damage by 25% against poisoned targets; Predator increases physical damage by 20% against slower targets. Corrosive Venom makes Poison attacks super effective against Rock while retaining the defender’s other type interactions; AI effectiveness scoring uses the same rule.

Spearow-family effects: Ruthless doubles critical chance against a target with a major status; Short Temper raises Attack one stage when a move misses (type immunity does not count); Relentless adds 10% damage after each successful damaging strike against the same target, up to 30%, including hits on Substitute. Switching or using a non-damaging move resets the stacks; a miss does not add a stack. Piercing Beak ignores 25% of Defense with Flying attacks; Sniper increases critical damage by 50%.

Pidgey-family effects: Tailwind raises Speed one stage on entry; Predator boosts physical damage by 20% against a slower target; Keen Eye prevents accuracy reductions; Piercing Beak ignores 25% of the defender's Defense for Flying moves; Gale Wings gives Flying moves +1 priority at full HP. Predator compares effective battle speeds. The existing type-based physical/special categories apply.

Rattata-family effects: Scavenger restores 1/4 maximum HP after a direct move KO; Strong Jaw boosts physical damage by 15%; Cornered raises Attack one stage the first time HP falls below one third in a battle; Hustle boosts physical damage by 25% and reduces its base accuracy by 15 percentage points; Poison Heal replaces poison damage with 1/8 maximum HP healing. ## Shared rules and menus

- Physical/special uses this game's Gen 1 type categories. Contact is a separate explicit move list in `tools/generate_ability_moves.py`, compiled to `data/ability_move_metadata.asm`; ranged physical moves such as Earthquake do not trigger contact reactions.
- Chance-based attack and reactive abilities roll once per move, not once per strike. Shell Guard specifically responds to each surviving critical strike, while Spiny Armor damages on each eligible hit. The 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% ability proc probabilities use exact rejection-sampled percentages.
- Substitute-protected targets do not trigger defensive reactions. Eligible reactions can still trigger on the hit that knocks out the ability holder; stat boosts for a hit recipient require it to survive. Poison Leaf, both Poison Point variants and Toxic Shell inflict ordinary poison, not Toxic. Type/status immunities and the existing Curse/poison exclusion remain enforced.
- Threshold once-per-battle flags, Hot Temper and Confident persist when switching. Quick Reflexes alone refreshes on a new entry.
- Damage multipliers normally apply to ordinary move damage; Measured and Thick Hide also reduce applicable fixed-damage moves, Counter and Bide. One-hit-KO moves, confusion self-damage and residual damage are unaffected. Multiple relevant damage factors combine multiplicatively with integer rounding.
- HP recovery rounds down, has a minimum of 1, caps at maximum HP and never revives.
- Transform keeps the user's own ability. Nature and ability slot metadata remain separate.
- Party menu **ABILITY** shows the selected Pokémon's current ability. Battle inspection remains **SELECT**, **LEFT/RIGHT** to switch side and **B/SELECT** to close. All new effects have descriptions.
- The trainer AI discourages Will-O-Wisp against Water Veil.
- Ability Capsules now work on every species in the table, changing to a different slot while preserving nature. Changing into or out of Ancient recalculates HP immediately. Nature Capsules preserve the ability slot. Species without a pool still reject Ability Capsules without consuming one.

## Storage

Ability descriptions and Resourceful PP routines are in the expanded ability bank, leaving room for additional battle mechanics and ability descriptions. Pool lookup uses a 16-bit offset, so it can address more than 255 ability slots. The saved ability slot and Pokémon record sizes are unchanged. Rollout, Slowpoke and Deep Sleep mechanics reserve transient bytes at the bottom of the stack allocation, leaving 173 stack bytes with the same stack top.

## Validation

Both ROMs are built and checked with `tools/test_early_abilities.py` and the existing prototype ability tests. Coverage includes all 500 species/slot combinations, both sides of battle, Pidgey-family entry, priority, accuracy and damage paths, Rattata-family damage, accuracy, knockouts, poison and low-HP triggers, exact chance boundaries, contact/Substitute rules and Ancient's native wild/capture/capsule/PC/daycare/save paths. Saved struct lengths, absolute nature scratch addresses and the stack top remain unchanged. A complete manual playthrough has not been performed.
