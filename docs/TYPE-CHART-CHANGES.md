# Type chart changes

- Ground attacks deal half damage against Ice.
- Poison attacks deal neutral damage against Bug.

Both changes apply to player and enemy attacks and the shared AI effectiveness calculation. All other matchups are preserved, including Bug being super effective against Poison and Flying dealing neutral damage to Ice.

Water/Ice Dewgong, Cloyster and Lapras, plus Ice/Psychic Jynx, now resist Ground. Articuno remains immune through Flying. Bug/Poison Pokémon now resist Poison; Paras takes 2× instead of 4×; Bug/Ghost Parasect now resists Poison. Pure Bug and Bug/Flying take neutral Poison damage.

No species, moves, stats or save format changed. This patch includes all previous sprite, species, move, encounter and trainer changes.
