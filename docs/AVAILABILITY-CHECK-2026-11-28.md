# Pokémon availability check — 28 November 2026

The current roster has 199 species. The previous 183-species [availability report](POKEMON-AVAILABILITY.md) identified 175 species obtainable individually in each version, including postgame Mewtwo and diploma-gated Mew, and eight unique variants without a player-acquisition route. The original wild tables have not changed since that review. Subsequent replacement pools leave at least 89% of the original encounter slots in each affected habitat, and the eight variants below do not occur in the current encounter pools, wild tables or evolution targets.

The 16 species added since that report have acquisition paths in both Red and Blue. Their base forms occur in the generated encounter pools, and their evolutions are present in the current evolution tables:

| Acquisition | Subsequent evolution |
| --- | --- |
| Tyker: Route 10 or Power Plant | Straigar, level 40 |
| Phanpy: Route 9 or Rock Tunnel 1F | Donphan, level 25 |
| Embear: Route 3; Ursear: Pokémon Mansion 1F or 2F | Embear → Ursear at level 24 → Volkuma at level 40 |
| Hoppip: Routes 5 or 6 | Skiploom at level 18 → Jumpluff at level 27 |
| Mareep: Routes 2 or 10 | Flaaffy at level 15 → Ampharos at level 30 |
| Garmic: Seafoam Islands B1F or B2F | Fangrir, level 30 |
| Eevee: Route 7 or other existing sources | Umbreon, Moon Stone |

This gives **191 species with identified player-acquisition paths and eight without**. The eight unavailable variants are:

- Unique Blastoise
- Koga's Grimer
- Erika's Ivysaur
- Giovanni's Nidoking
- Brock's Onix
- Surge's Raichu
- Erika's Tangela
- Erika's Venusaur

These variants appear in trainer teams, but no capture, gift, trade or evolution source was identified. The numbers describe whether each species is individually obtainable; they do not promise that mutually exclusive choices can all be acquired in one playthrough. This is a source and table audit building on the earlier report, not a fresh complete playthrough.
