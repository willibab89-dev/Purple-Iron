# Happy Ditto and Fighting variants

These changes build on all earlier sprite, species, move and TM patches. Species count remains 183; existing 183-species saves remain compatible.

Stats below are HP / Attack / Defense / Speed / Special.

## Happy Ditto

Normal; 80 / 48 / 48 / 48 / 48. Starts with Transform, keeps automatic entry transformation, learns no additional moves or TMs, and does not evolve. Transform retains its own HP. Its existing 1% level-30 Pokémon Mansion encounters remain.

## Fighting Geodude

Pure Fighting; 70 / 105 / 100 / 35 / 45. Does not evolve.

Starts with Tackle and Harden. Learns Low Kick at 9, Comet Punch at 17, Counter at 25, Rock Tomb at 33, Bulk Up at 41 and Mega Punch at 49. Mega Punch is Fighting-type in this game.

TM compatibility: Mega Punch, Body Slam, Take Down, Double-Edge, Submission, Counter, Seismic Toss, Rage, Rest, Rock Slide, Substitute, Strength, Facade, Bulk Up and Rock Tomb. No Earthquake, Dig, Fissure, Fire Blast, Selfdestruct or Explosion.

## Fighting Goldeen

Pure Fighting; 65 / 95 / 65 / 95 / 60. Does not evolve.

Starts with Peck and Tail Whip. Learns Horn Attack at 9, Supersonic at 17, Pierce at 25, Waterfall at 33, Focus Energy at 41 and Agility at 49. Pierce is Fighting-type, 70 power, 100 accuracy and 15 PP. No kick moves.

TM compatibility: Horn Drill, Take Down, Double-Edge, Bubblebeam, Water Gun, Submission, Seismic Toss, Double Team, Swift, Skull Bash, Rest, Substitute, Surf, Facade and Agility. No Ice Beam, Blizzard, Earthquake, Rock Slide or Bulk Up.

Both Fighting variants retain their existing 1% encounters in habitats containing their originals. They have independent evolution and level-up lists; ordinary Geodude and Goldeen are unchanged.

Existing Pokémon retain previously learned moves. Stat changes take effect when stats are recalculated, such as on level-up. The final review of earlier species for newly added moves remains deferred.
