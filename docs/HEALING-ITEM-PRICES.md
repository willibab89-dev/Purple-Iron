# Healing item prices

The early healing tier now starts at ¥200. Drinks cost roughly the same per HP as Super Potions, rather than making regular medicine obsolete on reaching Celadon. Healing amounts are unchanged.

| Item | HP restored / effect | Previous price | New price |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Potion | 20 HP | ¥300 | ¥200 |
| Super Potion | 50 HP | ¥700 | ¥600 |
| Fresh Water | 50 HP | ¥200 | ¥600 |
| Soda Pop | 60 HP | ¥300 | ¥750 |
| Lemonade | 80 HP | ¥350 | ¥1,000 |
| Burn Heal | Cures burn | ¥250 | ¥200 |
| Ice Heal | Cures freeze | ¥250 | ¥200 |

Antidote remains ¥100. Awakening and Parlyz Heal remain ¥200. Full Heal remains ¥600.

Hyper Potion (200 HP, ¥1,500), Max Potion (all HP, ¥2,500), Full Restore (all HP and status, ¥3,000), Revive (half HP, ¥1,500), Max Revive (all HP, ¥4,000) and First Aid (half maximum HP for every living party member, outside battle, ¥10,000) retain their prices. PP restoration items and capsules are unchanged. Selling prices still use the normal half-price rule.

The vending machine's displayed prices, affordability check and actual charge now agree. The affordability check reads the same BCD price record used for payment. The price column has been moved one tile left so ¥1,000 fits inside the menu border. Purchases fail without charging money when funds are insufficient or the bag is full.

Existing saves remain compatible. Shops and vending machines use the new prices immediately.
