# Unique Marowak and both Nidoking variants

Cumulative Red/Blue patches include all earlier sprites, species, move changes and TM changes. Species count remains 183 and move count remains 236 including Struggle. Existing 183-species saves remain compatible.

Stats are HP / Attack / Defense / Speed / Special. All three are standalone species with independent level-up lists and no evolution. Ordinary Marowak and Nidoking are unchanged.

## Unique Marowak: bone duelist

Ground/Fighting; 75 / 110 / 95 / 60 / 45.

Starts with Bone Club and Growl. Level 9 Leer, 17 Headbutt, 25 Focus Energy, 33 Pierce, 41 Bonemerang, 49 Counter, 57 Bulk Up.

TMs: Mega Punch, Mega Kick, Body Slam, Take Down, Double-Edge, Submission, Counter, Seismic Toss, Rage, Dig, Skull Bash, Rest, Substitute, Strength, Facade and Rock Tomb.

No Earthquake, Fissure, Hyper Beam, elemental special coverage, Will-O-Wisp or Curse. Keeps its existing rare level-40 encounter on Victory Road 2F. At that level its generated moves are Leer, Headbutt, Focus Energy and Pierce; Bone Club can be retained when raised or relearned. Bonemerang follows at 41.

## Giovanni's unique Nidoking: armored bruiser

Poison/Ground; 90 / 110 / 95 / 65 / 65.

Starts with Tackle and Leer. Level 9 Horn Attack, 17 Poison Tail, 25 Stomp, 33 Crunch, 41 Earthquake, 47 Poison Fang, 55 Megahorn.

The Poison moves use the audited revised order: Poison Fang is learned at 47, not Poison Tail. Giovanni retains his level-47 variant with Stomp, Crunch, Earthquake and Poison Fang. Poison Fang can badly poison; Poison Tail can be retained or relearned to exploit poisoned opponents.

TMs: Mega Punch, Mega Kick, Toxic, Horn Drill, Body Slam, Take Down, Double-Edge, Submission, Counter, Seismic Toss, Rage, Earthquake, Dig, Skull Bash, Rest, Rock Slide, Substitute, Strength, Facade, Rock Tomb and Poison Tail.

No elemental special coverage, Surf, Fissure, Hyper Beam, recovery beyond Rest or Speed setup.

## Second unique Nidoking: fast poison-and-horn attacker

Poison/Ground; 75 / 95 / 80 / 100 / 70.

Starts with Tackle and Leer. Level 9 Horn Attack, 17 Double Kick, 25 Toxic, 33 Poison Tail, 41 Pierce, 49 Megahorn, 57 Agility.

TMs: Mega Punch, Mega Kick, Toxic, Horn Drill, Take Down, Double-Edge, Submission, Counter, Seismic Toss, Rage, Dig, Swift, Skull Bash, Rest, Substitute, Strength, Facade, Poison Tail and Agility.

Toxic enables Poison Tail's guaranteed critical hit against poisoned targets. Critical hits follow the existing Gen 1 rules and ignore stat stages. Dig supplies optional Ground STAB; no Earthquake, Fissure, Rock Slide, elemental special coverage, Surf, Hyper Beam or Bulk Up. Keeps its existing rare level-30 encounter in Safari Zone Area 1. At 30 its generated moves are Leer, Horn Attack, Double Kick and Toxic; Poison Tail follows at 33 and is also available through its existing TM.

## Updating

Apply the matching BPS to an original, unmodified Red or Blue ROM. Back up saves. Existing Pokémon retain previously learned moves; changed stats appear when recalculated, such as on level-up. Shin's move relearner supplies eligible missed level-up moves.

Newly encountered unique Marowak uses Ground/Fighting. An already-owned specimen can retain its old saved Ground/Ground types; this patch does not migrate existing party or boxed records. The save layout is unchanged.

Both versions run the existing species/header/type, generated-move/PP, evolution-pointer, TM, encounter, save and battle-mechanics checks. BPS application is compared byte for byte with the built ROMs. Broad redistribution of newly introduced moves to earlier species remains deferred until the final review.
